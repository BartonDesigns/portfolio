Brewing madly together since 2007

The Mad Zymurgists are a homebrew club in the Tri-Valley area of Northern California: Dublin, Pleasanton, Livermore, San Ramon, Danville, and everywhere in between. Beer, cider, mead, and the science of fermentation, shared over a pint.

Who we are

A club for anyone who ferments, or wants to start

Our members range from first-batch beginners to BJCP judges and professional brewers, and every level is welcome. Bring your latest brew to a meeting and get honest, friendly feedback. We are 21 and over, and guests are always invited.

🍺

Beer social, every meeting

Every meeting opens with 30 minutes of sharing homebrew and conversation. Qualified BJCP judges are on hand if you want structured feedback on your beer.

🏆

Competitions

We enter and organize local and national homebrew competitions, including the Oaktoberfest homebrew competition, and host regional judging for the National Homebrew Competition.

🧪

Learn the craft

Guest speakers, brewing experiments, recipe formulation, beer cloning, and group brew days that help extract brewers make the jump to all-grain.

🎉

Beyond the kettle

Brewery visits, bus trips, tastings, a December holiday party, and a regular booth at the Northern California Homebrewers' Festival.

Membership

Join the Mad Z's

Being a supporting member of the Mad Zymurgists has its benefits. Pay your dues online, print a copy of the Club Charter, and file the agreement page with our Secretary.

Individual $35 / year

Annual club membership for one adult. Full voting rights, meetings, events, and competitions.

Pay dues via PayPal
Family $50 / year
